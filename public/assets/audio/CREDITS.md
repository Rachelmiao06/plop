## Ambience and cues (added 2026-11-25)

Also Freesound, also Creative Commons 0 (each page opened and the licence checked
on 2026-11-25). Cut from the site's 128 kbps preview MP3s, made mono, filtered,
peak-normalised, faded, and re-encoded (loops at 64 kbps, one-shots at 96 kbps).
The two loops have their last seconds cross-faded into the start so they loop
without a seam. Chosen by description, tags and ratings: nobody listened to them
while picking.
